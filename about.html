About Mother Touch

Our Story

A Dedicated Home Nursing Service in Kerala

Mother Touch Home Care is a dedicated home nursing service in Kerala providing professional, compassionate, and reliable care for elderly and recovering patients.

We understand how difficult it can be to see a loved one struggle, and how hard it is to leave them when you live far away. That's why we exist — to bridge the gap between professional medical care and the warmth of family.

Every caregiver on our team is carefully selected, professionally trained, and personally vetted to ensure your family receives only the highest standard of in-home care.

Our Core Values

Compassion

Every client is treated with the same love and care we would give our own family members. Empathy is at the heart of everything we do.

Trust & Safety

Every caregiver undergoes thorough background checks and professional training before being placed with a family.

Commitment

We are consistently dependable. When a family relies on us, we show up — every single day, without exception.

Excellence

We continuously improve through supervisor visits, feedback, and training to maintain the highest standards of care.

Family-Centred

We keep families informed, involved, and reassured — especially NRI families who rely on us to be their eyes and hands at home.

Professionalism

Hospital-level medical competence combined with kind, respectful conduct in every patient interaction.
